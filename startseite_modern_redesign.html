How to Join

There is no application form and no entrance exam. Joining us starts the way most good things do — by showing up once and seeing whether it fits.

01

Come Over

Drop by one of our open evenings or write us a short message. You will be shown around, fed, and introduced to far too many names at once.

02

Take Your Time

Spend a semester with us as a guest. Join what interests you, skip what does not. Nobody is asked to commit to anything in the first weeks.

03

Stay

If it feels right, you become part of the house — with a room if you want one, a vote in our decisions, and a network that outlasts your degree.

Kontakt

Say Hello.

Questions about the house, a room, or one of our events? Write us, call us, or simply ring the doorbell — somebody is almost always in.
